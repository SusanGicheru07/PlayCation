What is {{game.type}}

Charades is a fun and interactive guessing game where one team member silently acts out a word or phrase while the rest of the team tries to guess what it is. The goal is to guess as many correct answers as possible before time runs out. It’s a perfect game for building teamwork, quick thinking, and communication skills.

How to Play:

  1. Form Teams: Players are divided into two or more teams.
  2. Start a Round: One team selects a player to act out the clue generated in the game.
  3. Act Without Speaking: The player must act out the word or phrase without talking or making sounds.
  4. Guessing: Teammates must guess the correct answer before time runs out.
  5. Scoring: A correct guess earns 20 points for that round.
  6. Winning: The team with the highest points after all rounds wins.

What to Do Here:

  • Click "Add Round" to create a new round with a hint for the acting player.
  • After acting, select the team that guessed correctly to declare the winner.
  • The system will automatically update the leaderboard with new scores.

Singing Challenge

What is the Singing Challenge?
The Singing Challenge is a fun and competitive game where teams compete by singing songs. The system automatically analyzes each performance based on duration, loudness, and pitch to assign points fairly.

How to Play:

  1. Form Teams: Teams take turns to record their singing performance.
  2. Upload Audio: Use the upload button to submit your team's singing audio.
  3. Automatic Scoring: The system will evaluate the performance and assign points.
  4. Winning: The team with the highest total points after all rounds wins.

What to Do Here:

  • Click "Add Round" to start a new singing challenge round.
  • Upload the recorded audio for evaluation.
  • View the automatic scoring results and check the leaderboard.
